# Nevika Cura - Enhancement Ideas

## ✅ Implemented

3. **Real-time Staff Notifications** - Bell icon with badge for new appointment alerts

## 🎯 High-Priority Enhancements

### Patient Experience
1. **Smart Appointment Reminders**
   - WhatsApp reminders 24h and 1h before appointment
   - Option to reschedule directly from reminder message
   - Add to calendar (Google/Apple) one-tap button

2. **Queue Position Tracker**
   - Real-time "You are #X in queue" display
   - Estimated wait time based on average consultation duration
   - Push notification when "2 patients ahead"

3. **Prescription Digital Wallet**
   - Store all prescriptions digitally
   - Medicine reminder with photo of actual medicine
   - Refill request with one tap
   - Share prescription with pharmacy via QR

4. **Family Health Hub**
   - Add family members under one account
   - Book appointments for family members
   - Shared health records (with consent)
   - Family health calendar view

5. **Health Score Gamification**
   - Daily health check-in rewards
   - Streak bonuses for regular checkups
   - Badges for completing health goals
   - Leaderboard for engagement

### Doctor/Staff Experience
6. **Voice Notes for Prescriptions**
   - Doctor dictates, AI transcribes
   - Auto-generates structured prescription
   - Reduces typing time by 70%

7. **Smart Schedule Optimizer**
   - AI suggests optimal appointment slots
   - Auto-block buffer time after complex cases
   - Predict no-shows and suggest overbooking

8. **Patient Context Cards**
   - Quick summary before consultation
   - Last visit highlights
   - Pending reports/tests
   - Risk flags (allergies, chronic conditions)

9. **Inter-clinic Referral System**
   - One-tap referral to specialist
   - Auto-share relevant records
   - Track referral status

10. **Daily Summary Dashboard**
    - Morning brief: Today's appointments, high-priority patients
    - Evening wrap: Completed consultations, pending follow-ups
    - Weekly analytics email
